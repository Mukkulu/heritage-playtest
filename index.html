Match-3 Combat Prototype

Same four colours -- what each one does is entirely up to what you pick.

You're a blacksmith's heir clearing a dungeon, one fight at a time, using nothing but what your gear lets a match do. Quick rundown below, or just dive in.

Also trying the open-world prototype? It wraps this same combat in a map to explore between fights — play it here.

How to play

  1. Swap two adjacent gems (drag or click-click). No match? It snaps back — free, no swipe lost.
  2. Match 3+ of a colour to trigger whatever that colour does — the icon on each gem shows you (e.g. ✖ damage, ▣ block, ♥ heal, ◆ mana).
  3. Match 4 or more (a line, an L, or a T) and it doesn't pay out right away — it leaves a special gem instead. Match that again later for a much bigger blast.
  4. Fuse two specials by swiping them into each other — always works, no colour needed, and hits harder still.
  5. Enemies telegraph: their next move and its timing are shown ahead of time. Your 3 swipes a turn are for setting up against it, not just reacting.
  6. Five fights back to back, no healing between them — health carries over. See how far you get.
